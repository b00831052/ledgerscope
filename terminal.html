RWA Credit Intelligence · Methodology
Methodology
How every number on this site is produced, and where it can be wrong.

1. Data sources

  • Fund value: CoinGecko market cap (units outstanding x NAV) for every token in the tokenized credit, treasury, money market, commodity, equity and real estate categories, plus a curated list of flagship funds filed elsewhere.
  • Platforms without a priced token (Maple, Huma, OpenTrade, KAIO, RealT): protocol value from DefiLlama.
  • Yields: DefiLlama yield pools, matched by contract address, then by symbol.
  • Contract control: read directly from the chain (upgrade slots, multisig thresholds, timelock delays, privileged roles).
  • Excluded: registry-only records that are not freely transferable tokens (e.g. Figure HELOC), and tokens under $1M.

2. LS Score (0-100)

LS = 100 x (0.42 x size + 0.40 x stability + 0.18 x resilience). Size is log-scaled and capped at $2B. Stability falls linearly to 0 at a 25% weekly value move. Resilience counts networks, capped at 3. Bands: Green 68+, Amber 48-67, Red under 48. A stress flag fires when an income fund loses 8% or more of its value in 7 days.

3. NAV gap (yield vs risk)

NAV gap = offered yield minus required yield, in basis points. RICH at -75 bps or lower, CHEAP at +150 bps or higher.

  • Treasury and cash wrappers: the assets are T-bills, so the required yield is the risk-free rate plus a small wrapper premium (0 to about 2%) for issuer, operational and liquidity risk, never less than risk-free minus 0.25% (fees).
  • Credit funds: risk-free rate plus a credit spread by rating, adjusted for seniority, liquidity and manager tier.
  • Unobserved yield: tokens that pay under 0.5% on-chain usually accrue income inside the NAV. They get no valuation call.
  • Unverified yield: a credit fund paying less than T-bills + 2% on-chain (T-bills + 0.5% for AAA/AA credit) is flagged unverified, not RICH. Its income most likely accrues off-chain. The gap is shown greyed out.

4. Loan books

Maple: every active loan from Maple's public API. Per pool: amount lent out, number of loans and borrowers, top borrower's share, rate-weighted average, collateral value (priced daily) divided by loans outstanding, and how far crypto collateral can fall before cover reaches 1.0x. A loan whose on-chain payment deadline has passed is flagged "past deadline". Centrifuge: pending redemption orders divided by tokens outstanding. A queue of 10% or more of a fund raises an alert.

5. Key Risk

Who can upgrade, mint, pause or freeze each token, read from the chain. A key held by a single address is not called unsafe (institutions use MPC custody), but its governance cannot be verified on-chain, so the grade is capped at C.

6. Track record

Every day each fund's band is written to a public ledger. Each day's entries are hash-chained to the previous day and committed to git, so a past grade cannot be edited without breaking the chain. Band changes appear on the Rating Actions page, and each fund profile shows its grade history. Outcome test: did the fund lose 20% or more of its value within 30 days? The backtest replays the same rules on 12 months of history and is labelled as such.

7. Limits

  • History covers funds that exist today (survivorship bias).
  • Value change mixes flows and NAV moves.
  • On-chain yield can miss income that accrues off-chain.
  • Curated fundamentals are reviewed by hand and carry a confidence level.

Independent research based on public data and proprietary models. Not investment advice, not a credit rating under any regulation, not a solicitation.

RWA Credit Intelligence · Inside the funds
Loan Books
Market value tells you how big a fund is. The loan book tells you what it owns. Maple: every active loan read from Maple's public API: who borrowed, at what rate, against what collateral, and whether any loan is past its on-chain payment deadline. Cover = collateral value / loans outstanding. Crypto drop to 1.0x = how far crypto collateral can fall before the cover hits 1.0x. Centrifuge: redemption orders waiting to be paid, as a share of the tokens issued through Centrifuge (the same fund can also exist on other platforms).
