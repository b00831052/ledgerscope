RWA Credit Intelligence · Launchscope module
Launch & Key Risk
Who can change, mint, pause or freeze each fund token, read directly from the chain: upgrade slots (EIP-1967), the admin's own code (Safe multisig threshold, timelock delay) and the token's privileged powers. A key held by a plain address (EOA) is not called unsafe: institutions often use MPC custody, which looks like one address on-chain. It means governance cannot be verified on-chain and must come from the issuer's disclosures, so it caps the grade at C. Contract-level view only; not an assessment of the fund's assets.
